ABOUT MINGWAY

More than recruitment. A trusted people partner.

Established in 1983, Mingway has more than 40 years of experience in manpower supply and recruitment.

Our journey began with a focus on connecting people with employment opportunities. Over the years, our experience has grown with the changing needs of employers, industries and workers.

Today, Mingway provides recruitment and workforce support designed to help companies find suitable people while giving job seekers professional guidance as they begin or develop their careers.

We believe good recruitment is about more than filling a vacancy. It is about understanding the employer's requirements, identifying suitable people and supporting a professional employment journey.

GLOBAL CAREER JOURNEY

Supporting careers across borders.

Mingway helps international talents explore employment opportunities in Malaysia while providing practical support throughout the transition into a new workplace and environment.

The journey can involve recruitment, matching, cultural adaptation, regulatory guidance and practical settling-in support. Each stage is approached with clear communication and professional assistance.

01International Recruitment

Connecting employers with suitable talent through structured recruitment and matching.

02Cultural Adaptation

Helping new employees understand Malaysian workplace expectations and adapt to a new environment.

03Regulatory Support

Providing practical guidance on relevant employment and administrative processes.

04Assisted Living

Where applicable, providing guidance around accommodation, local resources and settling into daily life.
